FAQ

Questions? Answered.

So what's the catch?

There isn't one. We build your website first with no deposit. Once the project is complete and you're happy with the design, you pay the agreed price and we launch it. We agree the price with you before we start, so there are no surprises.

How much will I pay once it's finished?

It depends on the size of your site: number of pages, products and features. After a quick chat we'll give you a clear, fixed quote upfront. You won't pay a cent of it until the site is done and you've approved it.

What if I don't like the design?

Tell us what you'd like changed and we'll refine it. The whole point of this offer is that you only pay once you're happy, so we work with you until the design is right.

Is it a real, working website or just a mockup?

A real, fully functional website. You'll click through every page, test the forms and, for Shopify stores, see your products and checkout working before you pay.

Should I choose WordPress or Shopify?

If you mainly sell products online, Shopify is usually the best fit. If you're a service business, a brand or you publish content, WordPress is usually better. Not sure? Pick "Not sure" on the form and we'll recommend the right one.

What about hosting and my domain name?

We'll help you get set up, whether you already have a domain and hosting or are starting from scratch. Shopify includes hosting in its plan. For WordPress we'll recommend reliable hosting and handle the setup for you.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project. We'll give you a clear timeline at the start, and keep you updated as we build.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. The same offer applies to redesigns. We'll build your new site, and you only pay once it's finished and you're happy with it.
